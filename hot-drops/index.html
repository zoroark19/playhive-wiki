Starfire Crown

Starfire Crown was a hat purchasable with 5 gift tokens. Resembling an iron fireplace infused with mystic elements, it was the first hat to generate particles.

As Hive's first hot drop, only 500 units were available, and it is no longer obtainable.

Trivia

  • The sale only lasted for 5.28 seconds.
  • Custom Servers could not be created around release due to the shear amount of players on at the time.
  • The Hot Drop Ad also appeared in Mini-Hubs, not only the main Hub.
  • It was the first hat to also feature pauldrons, a type of armor.
  • Players can see what edition (number) their hat was in their locker.
  • The Hive were surprised at how quickly they sold.
